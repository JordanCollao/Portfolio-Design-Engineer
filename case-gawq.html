The project

I designed the interactions and motion language for Gawq, a US news and content app built around reading. The challenge was to define one motion system that matched the brand's visual identity and stayed usable and clear.

My part covered interaction design for the main flows, UX motion built in After Effects and Bodymovin (exported as Lottie JSON), and micro-interactions that guide attention and give feedback.

A pull-to-refresh interaction in Lottie that gives clear feedback while the content updates. The motion is playful but stays within the brand.
