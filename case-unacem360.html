/context

The project

I designed the UX/UI for a landing page for UNACEM, a leading Peruvian cement and construction company. It showcases the company's digital presence with a clean, modern interface that communicates trust and professionalism across its regional markets.

The design focuses on a clear visual hierarchy, simple navigation and a layout that puts key information and calls to action first, so users find what they need quickly.
