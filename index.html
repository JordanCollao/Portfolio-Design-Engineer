about

Hi, I'm Jordan from Perú 🇵🇪

I'm a UX/UI Designer Senior & Interaction Designer focused on digital products where motion supports human-computer interaction, usability and clarity.

I specialize in interaction design, affordances and micro-interactions, using motion to guide user behavior, communicate system states and reduce friction in complex interfaces.

I follow an AI-first approach, using AI at the early stages of the UX/UI process — research synthesis, ideation, exploration of interaction patterns and validation of concepts — always grounded in UX principles and product constraints.
