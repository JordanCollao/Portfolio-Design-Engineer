A Lottie motion system for Cantera, a hardware-store platform: three animations of about 6 seconds that make the core flows easy to follow.

/context

The project

I created 3 Lottie animations (~6 seconds each) to explain the key functionalities of Cantera, a hardware-store platform. Each one walks users through a core interaction. They're lightweight and loop cleanly, so the platform's flows are easy to follow.

/product

Product selection and quantity

Walks users through product selection: browsing items and entering how many units they need of each one.

Lottie: product selection and quantity

/quote

Choosing a quote

A list view that shows how to compare quotes and pick the one that fits best.

Lottie: choosing a quote

/location

Store location and contact

Shows how to view the chosen hardware store on a map and tap the contact button to get in touch.

Lottie: store location and contact
