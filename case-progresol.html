App Design for Progresol Plus

B2B mobile app connecting hardware sellers with retailers across Perú. End-to-end UI design for a Grupo UNACEM supply network, on iOS and Android.

Spec sheet

App Design (UI)
Period

/context

The project

Grupo UNACEM operates one of Perú's largest construction supply networks. Sellers and retailers needed a way to coordinate orders, manage credit lines, and track shipments without relying on phone calls and spreadsheets, which was how most of the network still worked.

The app had to work on whatever device the seller had, often an older Android phone, and stay usable in low-connectivity areas where many of the country's hardware stores operate.

/approach

App design

I led UI design across the iOS and Android product surfaces, working closely with PMs and engineering on the order flow, credit dashboard, catalog browsing, and shipment tracking screens.

Key decision: information density over visual flair. These are professional users completing dozens of orders per day, so the system uses high-contrast typography, clear data tables and predictable navigation.

Catalog, checkout and confirmation
/interface

Screens & flows

Motion stayed functional and only gives feedback on state changes. The credit dashboard and shipment tracking screens let sellers check the state of their operation quickly.

Home screens: Progresol 2.0 and independent stores
/outcome

Result

The app is in production across the UNACEM network, available on App Store and Google Play. The design system from this project is still the base for other B2B products in Grupo UNACEM.
